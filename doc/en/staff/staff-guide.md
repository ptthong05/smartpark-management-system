# STAFF USER GUIDE - SMARTPARK

## 1. Overview
The Staff Portal is designed for gate operators and security guards to manage vehicle entries, spot allocations, payments, and system alerts.

## 2. Standard Workflows

### 2.1. Vehicle Entry & Spot Assignment
1. Vehicle arrives at entry gate; AI scans the license plate.
2. Staff verifies the detected license plate on screen.
3. System shows available spots (`AVAILABLE`).
4. Staff selects an appropriate spot (status changes to `ASSIGNED`).
5. Staff verbally guides the driver to the assigned spot.
6. Barrier opens to let vehicle enter.

### 2.2. Vehicle Exit & Payment
1. Vehicle arrives at exit gate; AI scans plate and matches active session.
2. System calculates parking fee based on duration and rate policy.
3. Staff confirms cash or QR payment.
4. Barrier opens upon successful payment confirmation.
