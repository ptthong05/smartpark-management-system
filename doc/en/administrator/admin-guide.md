# ADMINISTRATOR USER GUIDE - SMARTPARK

## 1. Overview
The Administrator has full system permissions to manage parking facilities, cameras, rates, staff accounts, and view revenue analytics.

## 2. Key Responsibilities
1. **Parking Area & Spot Management**:
   - Create and organize floors and areas (Basement, Zone A, Zone B).
   - Add, edit, or set parking spots out of service.
2. **Camera Management**:
   - Register entry, exit, and basement cameras with their respective RTSP streams.
   - Map cameras to corresponding parking spots.
3. **Rate Management**:
   - Configure parking fees (default: 20,000 VND first hour, 10,000 VND subsequent hours).
4. **Staff Account Management**:
   - Create, lock, or update staff accounts.
5. **Analytics & Reports**:
   - Access real-time overview dashboard and revenue reports.
